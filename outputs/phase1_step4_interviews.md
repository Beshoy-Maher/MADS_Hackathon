# Phase 1, step 4: Customer interviews (Mom Test) for 'SQL Interview Prep Workbook for Junior Analysts'

## Method
- Talk about their life and past behaviour, not about our idea.
- Ask about specifics in the past, not opinions about the future ('would you buy...?').
- Talk less, listen more. No pitching during the interview.
- Compliments and 'sounds great' are not evidence. Commitments (time, money, intro) are.

## Interview guide
1. Tell me about the last time you tried to prepare for a SQL interview.
2. What did you do when you couldn’t figure out how to solve a practice SQL problem?
3. How did you find real interview questions to practice with?
4. What have you tried to improve your SQL interview skills in the past 3 months?
5. How much time have you spent searching for or practicing SQL interview questions?
6. How much money have you spent on interview prep resources in the last year?

**What counts as a strong signal:**
- Spent time or money on interview prep (e.g., courses, books, platforms)
- Asked for help from peers, mentors, or online communities
- Tried solving real interview questions from job postings or platforms like LeetCode, HackerRank, or Glassdoor
